# Sakura

## Platform
Web; responsive single-viewport experience for desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers.

## Stack
Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript (`index.html`, `style.css`, `app.js`).

## Product Purpose
Test8 (`sakura`) is an original, peaceful digital sanctuary inspired by a traditional Japanese waterfront shrine surrounded by blooming sakura trees and distant mountains.

## Layered Visual Architecture
1. **Sky & Distant Mountains**: Day and night horizon layers (`1920 × 1080` WebP from `1376 × 768` native artwork) featuring a gentle spring sky, Mount Fuji, and a moonlit starry night sky.
2. **Wordmark (`sakura`)**: Large serif wordmark (`Cormorant Garamond`) placed behind the shrine roof and sakura branches, with staggered entrance and subtle breathing motion.
3. **Shrine & Sakura Trees**: Transparent RGBA cutout layers (`shrine-day.webp` and `shrine-night.webp`) with natural blossom and roofline edges, warm illuminated lanterns at night, and pond reflections.
4. **Foreground Garden Bank**: Transparent RGBA cutout (`foreground-day.webp` and `foreground-night.webp`) with moss, wildflowers, stepping stones, and fallen sakura petals.
5. **Interactive Atmosphere**: Dual-depth canvas sakura petals, pointer parallax, a day/night toggle, diffuse night fireflies, and an accessible breathing ritual dialog.
